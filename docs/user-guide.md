# User Guide

This guide provides instructions for using the **Oxygen Sports Academy Annual Contract Renewal Tracker**.

---

## 1. Authentication
* **Logging In:** Navigate to the login page, enter your email and password, and click **Sign In**.
* **Password Reset/Change:** If your account requires a password change, you will be redirected to the Change Password screen upon logging in. Input your current password followed by a new secure password.

---

## 2. Administrator Workflows

### Managing Relationship Managers (RMs):
1. Click **User Management** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add User** -> Enter Employee ID, Name, Email, Department, and a temporary password -> Click **Create**.
3. To deactivate an RM, toggle their status switch to **Inactive**. RMs marked inactive cannot log in.

### Managing Contracts:
1. Navigate to **Contracts** -> Click **Add Contract**.
2. Complete all form sections:
   * **Section A:** Academy details.
   * **Section B:** Dates, status, and contract value.
   * **Section C:** Equipment categories and supply frequency.
   * **Section D:** Assigned relationship manager.
3. Click **Save Contract**. Admins can assign contracts to any active RM.

---

## 3. Relationship Manager Workflows

### Viewing Your Portfolio:
* RMs are restricted to their own portfolio. The dashboard totals, lists, alerts, and reports automatically filter to show only contracts assigned to your employee ID.

### Creating and Updating Contracts:
1. Click **Add Contract** -> Submit details. The system automatically assigns the contract to you.
2. To modify contract terms, open the contract details page and click **Edit**. Update price revisions, quantities, or logistics notes, then click **Save**.
3. RMs cannot reassign contracts to other managers.

---

## 4. Alerts and Reports

### Monitoring Alerts:
* Navigate to the **Alerts** page to view contracts categorised as **Critical** (< 7 days left) or **High Risk** (< 30 days left). Use this list to prioritize renewals.

### Generating Reports:
1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Filter the table by contract status or relationship manager (for Admins).
3. Click **Export CSV** to download the report for administrative review.
