Act as a Senior Product Designer, Senior Frontend Engineer, and SaaS Dashboard Architect.

Build a complete modern enterprise-grade frontend application for a company called Oxygen Sports.

PROJECT NAME:
Academy Annual Contract Renewal Tracker

BUSINESS CONTEXT:

Oxygen Sports is a sports equipment supplier that provides cricket equipment, football gear, badminton equipment, sports apparel, training accessories and athletic products to sports academies, schools and clubs.

The company manages annual supply contracts with these academies.

Currently contracts are tracked manually using spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages and phone calls.

This creates problems:

• Missed renewal deadlines
• Lost contracts to competitors
• Poor visibility of upcoming expiries
• No centralized data management
• Difficult reporting and analytics
• No automated tracking system

The goal of this application is to help Oxygen Sports monitor academy contracts, track expiry dates, manage relationship managers, monitor price revisions and generate business reports.
